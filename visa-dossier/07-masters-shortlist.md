## Warnings

- **Degree length:** a 3-year Tunisian licence (180 ECTS) can be refused by some programmes (USAL requires 240 ECTS). Confirm your degree qualifies in each coordinator email.
- **B2 at application:** most public programmes want the B2 certificate with the application (UCM accepts no promises). UPV (Sistemas Software, Análisis de Datos) and UPM avoid this.
- **Avoid títulos propios** (non-official degrees) and English-taught programmes. They don't justify a Spanish course.
- **Non-EU tuition (one year):** UC3M €9,288 · UAM €5,079 · UPV about €4,241. This affects the money you'll need to show for the master's visa.
- Start the **Spanish Ministry grade-average equivalence certificate** (*declaración de equivalencia de nota media*) now.
