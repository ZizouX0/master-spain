# Business master's: proofs obtainable by 1 December 2026 without Spanish B2

For a Tunisian business graduate following the same plan: a Spanish course in Spain from February 2027, then an official in-person master's taught in Spanish from September/October 2027. Researched 30 September 2026.

Marks:
- **V** = checked on the university's official page, its official rules, or the RUCT (Spain's official register of university degrees);
- **I** = inferred;
- **S** = seen only in a search snippet.

## Not usable

- Universidad Europea, Nebrija, CEU San Pablo, CUNEF, Deusto and UAX all require B2 at admission.
- Loyola's admission tests run from January.
- Villanueva and Abat Oliba programmes are hybrid.
- The UCJC business masters are online.
- IE, ESADE, IESE and Comillas ICADE teach in English.
- UPV's Responsabilidad y Sostenibilidad Corporativa is online.

## Suggested combination

- **Main:** ESIC MU Dirección de Marketing. It gives a written admission by email, and the €2,000 is refunded if the visa is refused. The family's proof of funds must show they can pay its tuition.
- **Backup:** UNIE's conditional admission, in person, in Madrid.
- **Public-university document:** the UPV Fase 0 receipt (MUGEPS + one Spanish-B2 master), or Zaragoza's "autorización de acceso".

The same rules as the main plan apply:
- one main master's and one backup;
- everything in the same field and taught in Spanish;
- dates and money consistent across all documents;
- a go / no-go check around 20–25 November.
