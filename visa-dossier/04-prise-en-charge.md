# Proof of money: blocked account, sponsorship letter (prise en charge) and supporting documents

- Legal minimum: **100% of IPREM = €600 per month** of stay (2026).
- What's done in practice in Tunis (agency checklist): **€700 per month, blocked irrevocably**.
- **Aim for €700 × the number of months**, plus any tuition you haven't paid yet.

Only **you and/or your parents** can be sponsors. Nobody else is accepted.

## Step 1: the student file and the blocked account at the bank

1. Go to the bank and ask the **branch manager** to open a **student file** (*dossier scolaire / compte de scolarité*) with **authorisation to transfer the money to Spain**.
   - If **you** have an account: the file is opened in your name.
   - If the money is in a **parent's** account: go to *their* bank. The usual solution is an account linked to the parent's account; the manager will explain.
2. Put **at least €700 × the number of months** into it (in dinars at the bank's rate), plus any tuition not yet paid, **blocked irrevocably**.
3. Get the **blocking certificate** (*attestation de blocage*), which must state that the amount is irrevocably blocked. Then get the **apostille** (at a notary) and the **sworn Spanish translation**.

Example: 5 months of course → 5 × €700 = **€3,500 blocked**, plus any unpaid fees.

## Step 2: the parent's sponsorship letter (Attestation de prise en charge)

1. The parent fills in the **Attestation de prise en charge**. Use the official BLS form ([PDF](https://tunisia.blsspainvisa.com/assets/pdf/french/ATTESTATION%20DE%20PRISE%20EN%20CHARGE.pdf)) or the same text typed out below.
2. They **sign it at the municipality** (*légalisation de signature*), as the form requires.
3. An authorised notary adds the **apostille**.
4. A sworn translator (*traductor jurado*) produces the **Spanish translation**.
5. Attach the parent's supporting documents (list below).

## Text (follows the BLS form, with two optional lines that make it clearer)

> **ATTESTATION DE PRISE EN CHARGE**
>
> Je soussigné(e) **[Nom et prénom du parent]**, en qualité de **[père / mère]**, titulaire de la CIN n° **[numéro]**, délivrée le **[date]** à **[lieu]**, N° de téléphone **[numéro]**,
>
> m'engage à subvenir à toutes les dépenses relatives à l'hébergement, aux transports et aux frais d'hospitalisation ou soins médicaux de :
>
> mon fils / ma fille **[Nom et prénom de l'étudiant(e)]**, né(e) le **[date]**, titulaire du passeport tunisien sous le N° **[numéro]**,
>
> pendant toute la durée de son séjour en Espagne du **[date début]** au **[date fin]**, sans avoir recours aux aides publiques,
>
> *(optional line)* à raison d'un montant minimum de **[montant] €** par mois, dans le cadre de son cours intensif de langue espagnole à Estudio Sampere, **[ville]**.
>
> *(optional line)* Je déclare exercer la profession de **[profession]** auprès de **[employeur]** depuis le **[date]**, avec un revenu mensuel net de **[montant] TND**.
>
> Fait à **[ville]**, le **[date]**
>
> Signature (légalisée auprès de la Municipalité)

The dates must match the school letter exactly. The optional lines are true statements that save the officer from having to work these facts out.

## The sponsoring parent's supporting documents

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| **The student's birth certificate** (*extrait de naissance*), in French, **issued within the last 3 months** | Proves the parent–child link. Apostille and sworn translation. |
| **The parent's CIN (national ID card) or passport**, copy | |
| **Employee** | **3 latest payslips** and an **attestation de travail** (job title, start date, salary), both issued **within the last 3 months**, with sworn translation. |
| **Trader** | **RNE** (business register) extract, **patente** (tax card) and **business opening declaration**, with sworn translation. |
| **Retired** | Pension certificate, proof of receiving the pension, and **CNRPS/CNSS** history, with sworn translation. |
| **Bank statements** for the parent's account, stamped by the bank | At least **3 months, ideally 6**. **No translation needed.** |

## Signs of trouble to avoid

- A **large deposit in the last few weeks** with no explanation. If it's a real one-off (for example selling a car or a property), attach the proof of that sale.
- A balance that only just reaches the minimum.
- A sponsor whose income clearly can't support the family and the student at the same time.
- Names, CIN numbers or dates that differ between the sponsorship letter, the statements and the birth certificate.
