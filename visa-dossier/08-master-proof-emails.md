# Proof of the master's: the proofs you can get, and the emails to send

Replace every `[bracket]`. Attach to each email: your diploma, all transcripts and your CV (scans are fine for a first contact). Send from a personal email address that uses your real name.

## The proofs you can have by late November

| # | Proof | How | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinators' written replies | Emails 1–5 below. Print each reply with the sender, date and university email address visible. | Send this week |
| 2 | Printed programme pages | Pages showing that the master's is taught in Spanish, its Spanish-level requirement and its admission calendar | Now |
| 3 | **UPV Fase 0 pre-registration receipt** | Apply on upv.es (one application covers up to 4 masters), pay the fee, download the *justificante* | Opened 17 Nov last year; watch from ~10 Nov |
| 4 | UCM pre-admission letter | Email 3; the faculty asks for about 6 weeks | Request now |
| 5 | UAM Early Admission decision (if your average qualifies) | Email 4, then apply to posgrado.oficial@uam.es | 1 Oct 2026 – 8 Jan 2027 |
| 6 | Universidad Europea admission letter (optional, private) | Email 5, then test + interview | All year |
| 7 | SIELE/DELE registration | Book the exam; print the confirmation | October–November |

## Rules for using these proofs

- Put them in the file exactly as the university sent them. Never edit a reply or a letter.
- If a coordinator says your degree doesn't qualify, drop that programme. Don't mention it in the file.
- In the motivation letter, name as your main target a programme that **requires B2** (UAM or UCM). Use the UPV receipt as proof that you've applied.
