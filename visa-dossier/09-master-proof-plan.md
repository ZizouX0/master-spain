# The plan: proof of the master's by 1 December 2026

Prepared 28 September 2026 for Mohamed Aziz Dardouri. Three strategy agents proposed ideas, five research agents checked them on official sources, and a reviewer acting as the consular officer tested the result. Everything below was checked on an official page unless marked *to confirm*.

**The idea in one line:** the course is presented as the condition of a master's you are already joining, not as "a language course". In practice:

- your main proof is a written admission to a Madrid master's whose only condition is Spanish B2;
- your backup is an application receipt from a public university;
- the Spanish course is in the same city and long enough to reach B2;
- the bank file, the money and the dates all tell the same story.

## 1. The Spanish course

- **Estudio Sampere Madrid, long-term course**: 20 lessons a week (45 minutes each, so 15 hours a week) for **32 weeks**, **Monday 8 February to Friday 17 September 2027**, about 480 hours.
- **Price:** 2026 price €4,090 + €49 enrolment. Confirm the 2027 price.
    - It starts any Monday.
    - The visa letter is issued only after full payment of the course and accommodation.
    - If the visa is refused, everything is refunded except €350.
- **Sampere's status:** Cervantes-accredited and an accredited DELE/SIELE exam centre. Its own price list presents long-term courses as preparation for university: "A B2 level of Spanish is required to access Spanish universities".
- **Why 32 weeks:**
    - Going from A1–A2 to B2 takes about 400 guided hours or more.
    - Your permit then runs to about 2 October 2027 (end of course + 15 days).
    - So you file the master's permit in Madrid by about early August, after your SIELE result, with no gap before an October master's.
- **Filing deadline:** file at least 2 months before the course starts (RD 1155/2024 art. 36.1). For 8 February the last day is **Monday 7 December 2026**. The course must last at least 91 days.

**What UNIE's letter must say:**

- the committee has evaluated your file;
- "admisión condicionada" to the Máster Universitario en Ciberseguridad, **modalidad presencial** (the official register also lists online places);
- full-time, 60 ECTS;
- the start date (October 2027) and the price;
- the **only** condition is B2 (certificate or UNIE's own test), and the deadline for it.

**A wording rule:** never call the UPV receipt an "admission". It is a pre-registration (preinscripción).

## 3. Supporting documents to have by 1 December

- **Your current Spanish level, certified:**
    - SIELE Global in Tunis by 20 October (€145, at the Instituto Cervantes or España La Academia Española, Lac 2; results in 3 weeks or less); and/or
    - an Instituto Cervantes Tunis intensive module in October–November (last published price 590 TND for 60 hours), with its certificate.
- **DELE B2 registration in Madrid** for 22 May 2027:
    - online from **18 November**, €204 (2026 price), paid by international card;
    - keep the emailed *resguardo* (registration receipt);
    - it is also what allows the one extension of your course permit if needed (art. 55.5);
    - refunds only within 14 days.
- **MedTech letter**, stating:
    - your degree gives access to postgraduate studies;
    - your credits in ECTS (MedTech says 5 years = 300 ECTS, so it must state which unit your 292.5 is);
    - the language of instruction;
    - the exact degree title.

    Also ask for a list of your security-related courses: that is your link to cybersecurity.
- **Bank file, under Central Bank circular 2025-10, art. 6:**
    - money for a language course before your main studies can be transferred only if the bank registers **one file covering both the course and the master's**;
    - open it with UNIE's letter and/or the UPV receipt;
    - the bank file itself then becomes proof of your plan.
- **Two-year budget and sponsor proof:**
    - salary or tax certificates;
    - 6–12 months of bank statements;
    - the source of any large deposit.
    - **Year 1:** course €4,139 + blocked funds €6,300 (€700 × 9 months, about 25 January to 2 October) + return ticket + insurance.
    - **Year 2:** UNIE tuition (about €6–7k, *to confirm*) or UPV €4,240.80 (non-EU rate) + living costs.
- **One-page itinerary (*cronograma*):** each step with its date and the number of the document that proves it.
- **Other evidence:**
    - positive replies from coordinators only;
    - certificates of what you have done since graduating (jobs, internships, certifications).
- **Existing pack items:**
    - apostilled criminal record (B3);
    - medical certificate;
    - insurance;
    - accommodation;
    - sworn Spanish translations of every document not in Spanish (the embassy requires them).

## 5. After you arrive (the interviewer may ask)

1. Arrive around late January. The visa can take up to about 5 weeks.
2. The course runs 8 February – 17 September.
3. Take SIELE at Sampere in June or July (results in 3 weeks or less), then send the B2 certificate to UNIE.
4. Get the admission and pay the fees.
5. File the master's permit in Madrid by about early August, at least 2 months before your permit ends. Also ask UNIE whether it can file the application itself (art. 54.6).

**If that fails:**

- **Safety net:** UPV Sistemas Software, which has no language requirement.
- **Last resort:** use the one extension (it needs the DELE registration), or return to Tunisia and apply for the master's visa from there.

## 6. What the motivation letter and the interview must say

1. **One goal:** the MU en Ciberseguridad at UNIE, in person, October 2027. Present it as the specialisation of your software-engineering degree (cite your security courses and projects), with UPV Sistemas Software as the alternative.
2. **Why in Spanish, and why Spain:** give true, specific reasons (the programme's content, an official European degree, your career plan). Expect the question "Why not an English-taught master's, or Spanish lessons at the Cervantes in Tunis?"
3. **A realistic language path:**
    - your certified level today;
    - about 480 hours of course;
    - your SIELE date;
    - the exact status of each university document, in precise words.
4. **Two-year funding:** who pays, how much, and the bank file covering the course and the master's.
5. **The fallback:** if you don't reach B2 or aren't admitted, you return and apply from Tunisia. Know every date by heart, and answer in Spanish as far as you can.
