# Motivation letter (carta de motivación): template

**How to use it:**
- Replace everything in `[brackets]` with true facts that a document in your file can prove.
- Delete any sentence that doesn't apply to you. Don't keep a sentence you can't support.
- Keep it to **one page**, signed and dated. It's written in Spanish, so no translation is needed.
- Once you've filled in [06-questionnaire.md](06-questionnaire.md), I'll write the final version for you. This template shows the structure that convinces.

## What makes this letter work (and what to avoid)

- **Every sentence can be checked against a document in the file.** The dates, the hours, the amounts and the programme names all match exactly.
- **It answers the officer's real question:** "Why does this person need a language course in Spain, and what happens afterwards?"
- **It doesn't overpromise.** No phrases like "I will definitely return", no emotional appeals, no claims that can't be proved.
- **It's specific.** Name the master's programmes, the DELE session, the hours per week and the euro amounts.
- **Don't copy it word for word from someone else.** Officers see the same template many times. Your real details are what make it persuasive.
