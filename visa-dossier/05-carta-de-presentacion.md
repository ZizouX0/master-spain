## Before you submit: consistency check

Tick each item only once you've checked it against the actual documents.

- [ ] Your name is spelled **identically** everywhere (passport, form, school letter, insurance, translations).
- [ ] The **course dates** match on the form, the school letter, the insurance, the accommodation and the sponsorship letter.
- [ ] The insurance runs **from the day you arrive to after the course ends** (the permit lasts 15 more days after the course).
- [ ] Every Tunisian document has its **apostille and sworn Spanish translation**.
- [ ] The criminal record and medical certificate are **recent**.
- [ ] The blocking certificate shows at least **€700 × the number of months** (plus any unpaid fees), and there are no unexplained deposits.
- [ ] You're submitting **at least 2 months before** the course starts.
- [ ] You have a **copy of the whole file** for yourself.
